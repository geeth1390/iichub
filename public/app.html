How IICHUB works

IICHUB connects people who need a part made with prototyping machines at government centres and verified private labs, so you can book an hour on a real machine near you.

Find a machineList your equipment

If you want something made

  1. 1

    Describe your part

    Enter its size (length × width × height in mm), the material, and how precise it needs to be. Choose the city you're near, or "Anywhere in India".

  2. 2

    Pick a machine and a free hour

    Results show only machines big enough and precise enough for your part, nearest first, with the price per hour and the days that still have free slots. Open Details to see photos, how a job runs and the safety rules.

  3. 3

    Get a quote, then decide

    Booking holds the slot. The centre works out the cost (machine time, setup fee and material) and sends you a quote in My jobs. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

  4. 4

    Follow the job and collect

    My jobs shows each stage: queued, being made, quality check, ready to collect. The centre's address and phone number are shown once your booking is confirmed.

If you own a machine

  1. 1

    Create an owner account

    Sign up and tick "I own equipment".

  2. 2

    List your device

    Five short steps: where it is, what it can make, photos (at least one of the device), how a job runs with your safety rules, then review. You can save and finish later.

  3. 3

    We check it

    The IICHUB team reviews every listing. We approve it, or ask for changes with a note explaining what to fix. Nothing is public until it's approved.

  4. 4

    Take bookings

    Innovators see your area and distance. Your full address and phone are shared only with people who have booked. If you change a live listing, it's checked again before it goes back up.

Common questions

What does it cost?

Each machine shows its price per hour, plus any setup fee and minimum charge. Material is added in the quote. Some centres give students a discount, and the quote shows the exact total before you agree.

Do I pay online?

Not yet. The centre tells you how to pay when it sends your quote.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Until work starts, you can cancel from My jobs and the slot is released. After that, contact the centre.

Who runs the machine?

It depends on the machine. Each machine page says whether the centre's operator runs it or you run it after a briefing.

What files should I bring?

Each machine page lists the file types it accepts, for example STL or 3MF for 3D printers and DXF or SVG for laser cutters.

Is a private lab safe to use?

Every private lab is checked by the IICHUB team before it appears, and is marked "Private lab · verified". Each one publishes its safety rules on its machine page.
